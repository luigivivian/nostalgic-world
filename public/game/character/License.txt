Rig / clips used by src/game/PlayerTPS.tsx
-----------------------------------------
Root bone chain: pelvis -> thigh_[lr] -> calf_[lr] -> foot_[lr] -> ball_[lr]
Head bone:       Head  (first-person camera mount)
Clips:           Idle_Loop, Walk_Loop, Sprint_Loop, Jump_Start, Jump_Loop, Jump_Land
                 (52 clips total, incl. Pistol_*, Sword_*, Swim_*, fly*, Dance_Loop)
Model height:    ~180 model units; the controller normalises this and applies
                 playerModelConfig.scale (0.01 here -> 1.8 world units tall).
